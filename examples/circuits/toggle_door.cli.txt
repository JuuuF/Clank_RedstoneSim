# Composition: button-toggled 2x2 piston door. Contract: doc/redstone/circuits.md, "Toggle door".
#
# The 2x2 piston door (examples/circuits/piston_door_2x2.nbt pasted at 0 1 0: cells
# 4..5 1..2 0, input bus 0..9 1 2) driven by a T flip-flop: a copper bulb @BULB at 4 1 4
# read by a comparator @Q at 4 1 3 that points north into the middle of the bus (so both
# ends get enough strength). A stone button @B on the bulb's south face toggles it: each
# press opens or closes the door.
#   redsim-cli toggle_door.redsim.json < examples/circuits/toggle_door.cli.txt
new size 32 force
note "Toggle door: each press of @B toggles the bulb; the door opens 6 ticks after an opening press and is closed 8 ticks after a closing press"
import examples/circuits/piston_door_2x2.nbt at 0 1 0
set 4 1 4 copper_bulb[lit=false,powered=false]
place 4 1 3 comparator out=north
place 4 1 5 stone_button face=wall facing=south
label B 4 1 5 "button on the bulb"
label BULB 4 1 4 "state: copper bulb (lit = open)"
label Q 4 1 3 "comparator into the door bus"
label D1 4 1 0 "door cell, lower west"
label D4 5 2 0 "door cell, upper east"
group toggle_door 0 1 0 9 2 5 in @B B out @D1 DOOR
settle
prefab save toggle_door 0 1 0 9 2 5

target new toggle "press at 2: open at 8; press at 40: closing at 46, solid at 48; press at 80: open at 86"
target toggle do at 2 use @B
target toggle do at 40 use @B
target toggle do at 80 use @B
target toggle expect @BULB lit=true during 2..39
target toggle expect @Q power=15 at 4
target toggle expect @D1 block=stone during 0..7
target toggle expect @D1 block=air during 8..45
target toggle expect @D4 block=air during 8..45
target toggle expect @D1 block=moving_piston during 46..47
target toggle expect @D1 block=stone during 48..85
target toggle expect @D1 block=air during 86..100

check

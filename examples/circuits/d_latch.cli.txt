# Circuit: D latch (locked repeater). Contract: doc/redstone/circuits.md, "D latch".
#
# Module (box 0 1 0 .. 1 1 2, output east):
#   @D     0 1 0  data input: wire, drive it from the west (-1 1 0)
#   @Q     1 1 0  repeater out=east (delay 1): follows D while E is off, holds while on
#   @LOCK  1 1 1  repeater out=north into @Q's side: locks it while E is on
#   @E     1 1 2  enable input: wire, drive it from the south (1 1 3); on = hold
# Test harness: levers @LD (west of @D) and @LE (south of @E).
#   redsim-cli d_latch.redsim.json < examples/circuits/d_latch.cli.txt
new size 16 force
note "D latch: @Q follows @D (2 ticks) while @E is off; holds from 2 ticks after @E turns on"
place 0 1 0 redstone_wire
place 1 1 0 repeater out=east
place 1 1 1 repeater out=north
place 1 1 2 redstone_wire
label D 0 1 0 "data input: wire, driven from the west"
label Q 1 1 0 "output: the locked repeater"
label LOCK 1 1 1 "side repeater: locks Q"
label E 1 1 2 "enable input: wire, driven from the south (on = hold)"
group d_latch 0 1 0 1 1 2 in @D D in @E E out @Q Q
prefab save d_latch 0 1 0 1 1 2
# harness
place -1 1 0 lever face=floor
place 1 1 3 lever face=floor
label LD -1 1 0 "test lever: data"
label LE 1 1 3 "test lever: enable"
settle

target new latch "transparent while E is off; holds while E is on; follows again 4 ticks after E turns off"
target latch do at 2 use @LD
target latch expect @Q powered=true during 4..9
target latch do at 10 use @LE
target latch expect @Q locked=true at 12
target latch do at 14 use @LD
target latch expect @Q powered=true during 12..33
target latch do at 30 use @LE
target latch expect @Q powered=false at 34

target new setup "D must change at least 1 tick before E rises to be captured"
target setup do at 9 use @LD
target setup do at 10 use @LE
target setup expect @Q powered=true during 11..30

target new late "failure case: D changing in the same tick as E is not captured"
target late do at 10 use @LD
target late do at 10 use @LE
target late do at 30 use @LE
target late expect @Q powered=false during 0..33
target late expect @Q powered=true at 34

check

# Requirement example: a 2x2 door, two buttons, closes 5 s after the last press.
# Workflow and contract: doc/redstone/workflow.md; library entry: doc/redstone/circuits.md, "Timed door".
#
# Requirement (as given): "A 2x2 door that opens when either of two buttons is pressed
# and closes 5 seconds after the last press; pressing again while it is open keeps it
# open." Acceptance (formalized): door cells clear within 20 ticks of a press; closing
# starts 95..110 ticks after the last press; a press while open restarts the time;
# both buttons work.
# Design: buttons @B1/@B2 on an input wire (x=-1) -> fade_timer.nbt (at 0 1 6: the
# retriggerable part) -> its output repeater powers the stone 3 1 5 -> (a) repeater
# 3 1 4 into the door bus at once, (b) three delay-4 repeaters (24 ticks) into the bus
# at 7 1 3: the bus stays on 24 ticks longer (an off-delay; the timer's output is long,
# so the OR has no gap) -> piston_door_2x2.nbt (at 0 1 0).
# Both paths reach the bus through repeaters: a first build tapped the timer's output
# wire after it had joined the bus, so the bus fed itself back and the door never closed.
#   redsim-cli timed_door.redsim.json < examples/circuits/timed_door.cli.txt
new size 48 force
note "Timed door: press @B1 or @B2: the door opens 18 ticks later and closes 102 ticks after the last press"
import examples/circuits/piston_door_2x2.nbt at 0 1 0
import examples/circuits/fade_timer.nbt at 0 1 6
place 3 1 5 stone
place 3 1 4 repeater out=north
place 3 1 3 redstone_wire
place 4 1 5 repeater out=east delay=4
place 5 1 5 repeater out=east delay=4
place 6 1 5 repeater out=east delay=4
line 7 1 5 to 7 1 3 redstone_wire
line -1 1 4 to -1 1 10 redstone_wire
place -2 1 10 stone_button face=floor
place -2 1 4 stone_button face=floor
label B1 -2 1 10 "button 1"
label B2 -2 1 4 "button 2"
label TIMER 3 1 6 "fade timer output"
label D1 4 1 0 "door cell, lower west"
label D4 5 2 0 "door cell, upper east"
group timed_door -2 1 0 9 2 9 in @B1 B1 in @B2 B2 out @D1 DOOR
settle
prefab save timed_door -2 1 0 9 2 9

target new press "one press at 2: open from 20, closing starts at 104 (102 after the press), closed (solid) at 106"
target press do at 2 use @B1
target press expect @D1 block=stone during 0..19
target press expect @D1 block=air during 20..103
target press expect @D4 block=air during 20..103
target press expect @D1 block=moving_piston at 104
target press expect @D1 block=stone during 106..140

target new again "pressed at 2 and again (other button) at 60 while open: stays open until 162 (60 + 102)"
target again do at 2 use @B1
target again do at 60 use @B2
target again expect @D1 block=air during 20..161
target again expect @D1 block=moving_piston at 162
target again expect @D1 block=stone during 164..200

target new reopen "pressed at 2, and again at 150 after it closed: opens again at 168"
target reopen do at 2 use @B1
target reopen do at 150 use @B1
target reopen expect @D1 block=stone during 106..167
target reopen expect @D1 block=air during 168..251
target reopen expect @D1 block=stone during 254..300

check

# Circuit: analog 2:1 multiplexer (OUT = SEL ? B : A, strength kept). Contract:
# doc/redstone/circuits.md, "Analog multiplexer".
#
# Module (box 0 1 0 .. 5 1 4, output east):
#   @SEL  0 1 1  select: wire; drive it from the west (here a lever at -1 1 1)
#   @A    4 1 0  input A: an analog block (container …) or a block powered with the strength
#   @B    4 1 4  input B: the same
#   @OUT  5 1 2  comparator facing east: strength A while SEL is off, B while it is on
# CA (4 1 1, subtract, facing south) reads A and has SEL at its side through the repeater
# 3 1 1: A - 15*SEL. CB (4 1 3, subtract, facing north) reads B and has NOT SEL at its side
# (torch 2 1 3 on the stone 1 1 3, wire 3 1 3): B - 15*(NOT SEL). Both point into the
# stone 4 1 2; OUT reads it: the larger, i.e. the selected input. The repeater (2 ticks) and
# the torch (2 ticks) keep the two gates switching in the same tick (no glitch).
#   redsim-cli analog_mux.redsim.json < examples/circuits/analog_mux.cli.txt
new size 16 force
note "Analog multiplexer: comparator @OUT gives the strength of @A while @SEL is off, of @B while it is on (6 ticks after SEL, 4 after the selected input)"
source 4 1 0 barrel 5
source 4 1 4 barrel 11
place 4 1 1 comparator out=south mode=subtract
place 4 1 3 comparator out=north mode=subtract
place 4 1 2 stone
place 5 1 2 comparator out=east
place 3 1 1 repeater out=east
place 1 1 3 stone
place 2 1 3 redstone_wall_torch facing=east
place 3 1 3 redstone_wire
line 0 1 1 to 2 1 1 redstone_wire
place 1 1 2 redstone_wire
place -1 1 1 lever face=floor
place 6 1 2 redstone_lamp
label SEL -1 1 1 "select (lever; the module's port is the wire 0 1 1)"
label A 4 1 0 "input A (barrel)"
label B 4 1 4 "input B (barrel)"
label CA 4 1 1 "A - 15*SEL"
label CB 4 1 3 "B - 15*(NOT SEL)"
label OUT 5 1 2 "output: the selected strength"
group analog_mux 0 1 0 5 1 4 in 0 1 1 SEL in @A A in @B B out @OUT OUT
settle
prefab save analog_mux 0 1 0 5 1 4

target new select "A=5, B=11: OUT 5; SEL on at 10 → 11 at 16; A to 9 at 20 (unselected) changes nothing; B to 3 at 30 → 3 at 34; SEL off at 40 → 9 at 46; B to 14 at 50 changes nothing"
target select expect @OUT power=5 during 1..15
target select do at 10 use @SEL
target select expect @OUT power=11 during 16..33
target select do at 20 source @A barrel 9
target select do at 30 source @B barrel 3
target select expect @OUT power=3 during 34..45
target select do at 40 use @SEL
target select do at 50 source @B barrel 14
target select expect @OUT power=9 during 46..60

target new noglitch "SEL switched every 10 ticks with A=5, B=11: OUT goes straight from one value to the other, never 0"
target noglitch do at 10 use @SEL
target noglitch do at 20 use @SEL
target noglitch do at 30 use @SEL
target noglitch do at 40 use @SEL
target noglitch expect @OUT power>=5 during 1..56
target noglitch expect @OUT power=11 during 16..25
target noglitch expect @OUT power=5 during 26..35

target new extremes "A=15 is blocked fully while B=0 is selected (OUT 0); A=0, B=15 selected gives 15"
target extremes do source @A barrel 15
target extremes do container @B empty
target extremes do at 10 use @SEL
target extremes expect @OUT power=15 during 4..15
target extremes expect @OUT power=0 during 16..25
target extremes do at 30 container @A empty
target extremes do at 30 source @B barrel 15
target extremes expect @OUT power=15 during 34..45

target new sel1 "a 1-tick SEL pulse is lost: the torch ignores it, and CA misses the repeater's 2-tick pulse (R-003)"
target sel1 do at 10 use @SEL
target sel1 do at 11 use @SEL
target sel1 expect @OUT power=5 during 1..30
target sel1 expect @CB power=0 during 1..30

target new sel2 "a 2-tick SEL pulse: CB passes B for 2 ticks but CA misses its gate, so OUT is max(A, B) then (11 here), not B: SEL changes must last 3+ ticks"
target sel2 do at 10 use @SEL
target sel2 do at 12 use @SEL
target sel2 expect @CA power=5 during 1..30
target sel2 expect @CB power=11 during 14..15
target sel2 expect @OUT power=11 during 16..17
target sel2 expect @OUT power=5 during 18..30

target new sel3 "a 3-tick SEL pulse switches cleanly: CA 0 and CB 11 at 14-16, OUT 11 at 16-18"
target sel3 do at 10 use @SEL
target sel3 do at 13 use @SEL
target sel3 expect @CA power=0 during 14..16
target sel3 expect @CB power=11 during 14..16
target sel3 expect @OUT power=11 during 16..18
target sel3 expect @OUT power=5 during 19..30

check

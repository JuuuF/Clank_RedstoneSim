# R-003: short pulses and short gaps (doc/redstone/records/R-003-short-pulses.md)
#
# Part 1, player-timed pulses: columns of rows driven by a lever that is on for 1, 2 or
# 3 ticks from tick 2 (columns x=-24, -12, 0), or by an observer watching a lever flipped

# component: repeater delay 1 and 2, comparator, torch (on a stone the lever hangs on or

# 2-tick (1-tick lever pulse, delay 1), 3-tick (3-tick lever pulse, delay 1) or 4-tick

#   redsim-cli r003.redsim.json < experiments/redstone/R-003-short-pulses.cli.txt

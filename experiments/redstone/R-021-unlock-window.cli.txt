# R-021: how long may a chain of latches be unlocked? (doc/redstone/records/R-021-unlock-window.md)
#
# Four copies of a 4-latch chain (repeaters facing east, wire between, each with a lock
# repeater on its north side fed by one lock bus whose lever is on = all locked). A data
# lever at the west end goes on at 4 and off at 34; the bus is switched off for w ticks
# at 10, 30 and 50, with w = 2, 3, 4, 5 in the four copies.
#   redsim-cli r021.redsim.json < experiments/redstone/R-021-unlock-window.cli.txt
new size 64 force
note "R-021: a shared lock bus opened for 3-4 ticks moves a latch chain exactly one stage; 2 ticks: nothing; 5: two stages"
place -1 1 -24 lever face=floor
label D2 -1 1 -24

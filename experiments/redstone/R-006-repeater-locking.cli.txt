# R-006: what locks a repeater, and what happens to inputs while locked (doc/redstone/records/R-006-repeater-locking.md)
#
# Rows 5 blocks apart: a data lever D_x west of a repeater Q_x (out=east), and at Q_x's
# south side: a repeater (REP, PEND), a comparator reading a barrel of strength 1 (COMP1),
# wire, a wall torch, a stone with a lever on top, a redstone block (set at tick 2), an
# observer watching a lever (OBS).
#   redsim-cli r006.redsim.json < experiments/redstone/R-006-repeater-locking.cli.txt
new size 48 force
note "R-006: what locks a repeater (side repeaters and comparators only) and what a locked repeater does with its input"
place -1 1 -20 lever face=floor
label D_REP -1 1 -20
place 0 1 -20 repeater out=east
label Q_REP 0 1 -20
place 0 1 -19 repeater out=north
place 0 1 -18 lever face=floor
label S_REP 0 1 -18
place -1 1 -15 lever face=floor
label D_COMP1 -1 1 -15
place 0 1 -15 repeater out=east
label Q_COMP1 0 1 -15
place 0 1 -14 comparator out=north
source 0 1 -13 barrel 1
label S_COMP1 0 1 -13
place -1 1 -10 lever face=floor
label D_WIRE -1 1 -10
place 0 1 -10 repeater out=east
label Q_WIRE 0 1 -10
place 0 1 -9 redstone_wire
place 0 1 -8 lever face=floor
label S_WIRE 0 1 -8
place -1 1 -5 lever face=floor
label D_TORCH -1 1 -5
place 0 1 -5 repeater out=east
label Q_TORCH 0 1 -5
place 0 1 -3 stone
place 0 1 -4 redstone_wall_torch facing=north
label S_TORCH 0 1 -3
place -1 1 0 lever face=floor
label D_BLOCK -1 1 0
place 0 1 0 repeater out=east
label Q_BLOCK 0 1 0
place 0 1 1 stone
place 0 2 1 lever face=floor
label S_BLOCK 0 2 1
place -1 1 5 lever face=floor
label D_RBLOCK -1 1 5
place 0 1 5 repeater out=east
label Q_RBLOCK 0 1 5
label S_RBLOCK 0 1 6
place -1 1 10 lever face=floor
label D_OBS -1 1 10
place 0 1 10 repeater out=east
label Q_OBS 0 1 10
place 0 1 11 observer out=north
place 0 1 12 lever face=floor
label S_OBS 0 1 12
place -1 1 15 lever face=floor
label D_PEND -1 1 15
place 0 1 15 repeater out=east
label Q_PEND 0 1 15
place 0 1 16 repeater out=north
place 0 1 17 lever face=floor
label S_PEND 0 1 17
settle

target new lockers "a repeater or a comparator (even at strength 1) at the side locks; wire, a torch, a powered block, a redstone block, an observer do not"
target lockers do at 2 use @S_REP
target lockers do at 2 use @S_WIRE
target lockers do at 2 use @S_BLOCK
target lockers do at 2 set @S_RBLOCK redstone_block
target lockers do at 10 use @D_REP
target lockers do at 10 use @D_COMP1
target lockers do at 10 use @D_WIRE
target lockers do at 10 use @D_TORCH
target lockers do at 10 use @D_BLOCK
target lockers do at 10 use @D_RBLOCK
target lockers expect @Q_REP locked=true at 4
target lockers expect @Q_REP powered=false during 0..30
target lockers expect @Q_COMP1 locked=true at 0
target lockers expect @Q_COMP1 powered=false during 0..30
target lockers expect @Q_WIRE locked=false during 0..30
target lockers expect @Q_WIRE powered=true at 12
target lockers expect @Q_TORCH locked=false during 0..30
target lockers expect @Q_TORCH powered=true at 12
target lockers expect @Q_BLOCK locked=false during 0..30
target lockers expect @Q_BLOCK powered=true at 12
target lockers expect @Q_RBLOCK locked=false during 0..30
target lockers expect @Q_RBLOCK powered=true at 12

target new observer "an observer pulsing into the side does not lock"
target observer do at 2 use @S_OBS
target observer do at 3 use @D_OBS
target observer expect @Q_OBS locked=false during 0..20
target observer expect @Q_OBS powered=true at 5

target new unlock "input changes while locked are ignored; after unlocking the repeater catches up after its delay"
target unlock do at 2 use @S_REP
target unlock do at 10 use @D_REP
target unlock do at 20 use @S_REP
target unlock expect @Q_REP locked=true at 21
target unlock expect @Q_REP locked=false at 22
target unlock expect @Q_REP powered=false at 23
target unlock expect @Q_REP powered=true at 24

target new pending "locking while a tick is pending cancels it: the change is not delayed, it is dropped until unlock"
target pending do at 1 use @S_PEND
target pending do at 2 use @D_PEND
target pending do at 20 use @S_PEND
target pending expect @Q_PEND locked=true at 3
target pending expect @Q_PEND powered=false during 0..23
target pending expect @Q_PEND powered=true at 24

check

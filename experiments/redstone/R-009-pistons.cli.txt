# R-009: pistons as building blocks: push limit, what stops or breaks, sticky and
# slime behavior, quasi-connectivity (doc/redstone/records/R-009-pistons.md)
#
# Rows 3-4 blocks apart, pistons at x=0 facing east with a lever west of them: 12 and 13
# stones in front (PUSH12, PUSH13); stone then obsidian (OBSID); a barrel (BARREL); a stone
# with a torch beyond it (POP); a sticky piston with glazed terracotta (TERRA); a sticky
# piston with a slime block carrying a stone on top and a honey block beside it, resting
# on the stone floor (SLIMEFLOOR) or on obsidian (SLIME); a piston powered only through
# the space above it by a redstone block set diagonally (QCBUD) or by a repeater pointing
# into that space (QCREP); a piston pushing a redstone block towards a lamp (RBPUSH); a
# piston behind a repeater (REPDRV) and a sticky piston beside a torch (TORCHDRV).
#   redsim-cli r009.redsim.json < experiments/redstone/R-009-pistons.cli.txt
new size 64 force
note "R-009: pistons: 12-block limit, immovable and popped blocks, sticky and slime behavior, quasi-connectivity and BUD"
place -1 1 -28 lever face=floor
label L_PUSH12 -1 1 -28
place 0 1 -28 piston facing=east
label P_PUSH12 0 1 -28
fill 1 1 -28 12 1 -28 stone
label END_PUSH12 13 1 -28
place -1 1 -25 lever face=floor
label L_PUSH13 -1 1 -25
place 0 1 -25 piston facing=east
label P_PUSH13 0 1 -25
fill 1 1 -25 13 1 -25 stone
label END_PUSH13 14 1 -25
place -1 1 -22 lever face=floor
label L_OBSID -1 1 -22
place 0 1 -22 piston facing=east
label P_OBSID 0 1 -22
place 1 1 -22 stone
place 2 1 -22 obsidian
label F_OBSID 1 1 -22
place -1 1 -19 lever face=floor
label L_BARREL -1 1 -19
place 0 1 -19 piston facing=east
label P_BARREL 0 1 -19
place 1 1 -19 barrel
label F_BARREL 1 1 -19
place -1 1 -16 lever face=floor
label L_POP -1 1 -16
place 0 1 -16 piston facing=east
label P_POP 0 1 -16
place 1 1 -16 stone
place 2 1 -16 redstone_torch
label F_POP 2 1 -16
place -1 1 -13 lever face=floor
label L_TERRA -1 1 -13
place 0 1 -13 sticky_piston facing=east
label P_TERRA 0 1 -13
place 1 1 -13 white_glazed_terracotta
label F1_TERRA 1 1 -13
label F2_TERRA 2 1 -13
place -1 1 -9 lever face=floor
label L_SLIMEFLOOR -1 1 -9
place 0 1 -9 sticky_piston facing=east
label P_SLIMEFLOOR 0 1 -9
place 1 1 -9 slime_block
place 1 2 -9 stone
set 1 1 -8 honey_block
label TOP0_SLIMEFLOOR 1 2 -9
label TOP1_SLIMEFLOOR 2 2 -9
label HONEY_SLIMEFLOOR 1 1 -8
label S1_SLIMEFLOOR 2 1 -9
place 0 1 -5 piston facing=east
label P_QCBUD 0 1 -5
label RB_QCBUD 0 2 -4
label NB_QCBUD 0 1 -6
place 0 1 -1 piston facing=east
label P_QCREP 0 1 -1
place -1 1 -1 stone
place -1 2 -1 repeater out=east
place -2 1 -1 stone
place -2 2 -1 lever face=floor
label L_QCREP -2 2 -1
place -1 1 3 lever face=floor
label L_RBPUSH -1 1 3
place 0 1 3 piston facing=east
label P_RBPUSH 0 1 3
set 1 1 3 redstone_block
place 3 1 3 redstone_lamp
label LAMP_RBPUSH 3 1 3
place -1 1 18 lever face=floor
label L_SLIME -1 1 18
place 0 1 18 sticky_piston facing=east
label P_SLIME 0 1 18
set 1 0 18 obsidian
set 2 0 18 obsidian
place 1 1 18 slime_block
place 1 2 18 stone
set 1 1 19 honey_block
label TOP0_SLIME 1 2 18
label TOP1_SLIME 2 2 18
label HONEY_SLIME 1 1 19
label S1_SLIME 2 1 18
label S0_SLIME 1 1 18
place -2 1 22 lever face=floor
place -1 1 22 repeater out=east
place 0 1 22 piston facing=east
label L_REPDRV -2 1 22
label R_REPDRV -1 1 22
label P_REPDRV 0 1 22
place -1 1 25 stone
place -2 1 25 lever face=wall facing=west
place 0 1 25 redstone_wall_torch facing=east
place 1 1 25 sticky_piston facing=south
label L_TORCHDRV -2 1 25
label T_TORCHDRV 0 1 25
label P_TORCHDRV 1 1 25
settle

target new limits "12 blocks push, 13 do not; obsidian and block entities stop the piston; a torch in the way breaks"
target limits do at 2 use @L_PUSH12
target limits do at 2 use @L_PUSH13
target limits do at 2 use @L_OBSID
target limits do at 2 use @L_BARREL
target limits do at 2 use @L_POP
target limits expect @P_PUSH12 extended=true at 3
target limits expect @END_PUSH12 block=stone at 5
target limits expect @P_PUSH13 extended=false during 0..20
target limits expect @END_PUSH13 block=air during 0..20
target limits expect @P_OBSID extended=false during 0..20
target limits expect @F_OBSID block=stone during 0..20
target limits expect @P_BARREL extended=false during 0..20
target limits expect @F_BARREL block=barrel during 0..20
target limits expect @P_POP extended=true at 3
target limits expect @F_POP block=stone at 5

target new sticky "a sticky piston leaves glazed terracotta behind; slime carries what touches it (not honey); slime on the stone floor drags the floor and the push fails"
target sticky do at 2 use @L_TERRA
target sticky do at 20 use @L_TERRA
target sticky do at 2 use @L_SLIME
target sticky do at 20 use @L_SLIME
target sticky do at 2 use @L_SLIMEFLOOR
target sticky expect @F2_TERRA block=white_glazed_terracotta during 5..30
target sticky expect @F1_TERRA block=air during 21..30
target sticky expect @S1_SLIME block=slime_block during 5..20
target sticky expect @TOP1_SLIME block=stone during 5..20
target sticky expect @HONEY_SLIME block=honey_block during 0..30
target sticky expect @S0_SLIME block=slime_block during 23..30
target sticky expect @TOP0_SLIME block=stone during 23..30
target sticky expect @S1_SLIME block=air during 21..30
target sticky expect @P_SLIMEFLOOR extended=false during 0..30
target sticky expect @TOP1_SLIMEFLOOR block=air during 0..30

target new qc "power at the space above counts (quasi-connectivity), but only when the piston gets an update: it extends at the next update and stays extended until another one"
target qc do at 2 set @RB_QCBUD redstone_block
target qc do at 10 place @NB_QCBUD stone
target qc do at 20 set @RB_QCBUD air
target qc do at 30 break @NB_QCBUD
target qc do at 2 use @L_QCREP
target qc expect @P_QCBUD extended=false during 0..10
target qc expect @P_QCBUD extended=true during 11..30
target qc expect @P_QCBUD extended=false at 33
target qc expect @P_QCREP extended=false at 3
target qc expect @P_QCREP extended=true at 4

target new carry "a pushed redstone block powers its new neighbors only when it lands (tick +3)"
target carry do at 2 use @L_RBPUSH
target carry expect @LAMP_RBPUSH lit=false at 4
target carry expect @LAMP_RBPUSH lit=true at 5

target new sametick "a piston powered by a component that switches during the scheduled ticks moves in that same tick (block events come later in the tick); from a lever, one tick later"
target sametick do at 2 use @L_REPDRV
target sametick do at 2 use @L_TORCHDRV
target sametick expect @R_REPDRV powered=true at 4
target sametick expect @P_REPDRV extended=false at 3
target sametick expect @P_REPDRV extended=true at 4
target sametick expect @T_TORCHDRV lit=false at 4
target sametick expect @P_TORCHDRV extended=true at 3
target sametick expect @P_TORCHDRV block=moving_piston at 4

check

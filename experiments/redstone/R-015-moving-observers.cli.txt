# R-015: observers moved by pistons; sticky pistons pulling across a gap (doc/redstone/records/R-015-moving-observers.md)
#
# Rows: three observers (watching south, east, west) pushed one block east by a piston
# whose lever goes on at 2; an observer watching a lever (flipped at 2: it pulses 4..5)
# pushed at tick 4, while it is powered, with a repeater where its output will point; a
# sticky piston with a stone two cells ahead (one cell of air between), on for 2 ticks.
#   redsim-cli r015.redsim.json < experiments/redstone/R-015-moving-observers.cli.txt
new size 32 force
note "R-015: observers moved by pistons, sticky pistons pulling after extending into air"
# moved observers (piston pushes them east), facing south / east / west
place -1 1 -10 lever face=floor
place 0 1 -10 piston facing=east
place 1 1 -10 observer facing=south
label L_S -1 1 -10
label O_S 2 1 -10
place -1 1 -7 lever face=floor
place 0 1 -7 piston facing=east
place 1 1 -7 observer facing=east
label L_E -1 1 -7
label O_E 2 1 -7
place -1 1 -4 lever face=floor
place 0 1 -4 piston facing=east
place 1 1 -4 observer facing=west
label L_W -1 1 -4
label O_W 2 1 -4
# an observer moved while it is powered: it watches a lever north of it (flipped at 2, pulse 4..5); its piston fires at 4
place 1 1 0 observer facing=north
place 1 1 -1 lever face=floor
place 0 1 0 piston facing=east
place -1 1 0 stone
place -2 1 0 lever face=wall facing=west
place 2 1 1 repeater out=south
label L_WATCH 1 1 -1
label L_PUSH -2 1 0
label O_POW 2 1 0
label PROBE 2 1 1
# sticky pistons with a block one cell away (a gap): 2-tick pulse
place -1 1 4 lever face=floor
place 0 1 4 sticky_piston facing=east
place 2 1 4 stone
label L_GAP -1 1 4
label GAP_FROM 2 1 4
label GAP_TO 1 1 4
settle

target new moved "a pushed observer pulses 2 ticks after it lands, whichever way it faces"
target moved do at 2 use @L_S
target moved do at 2 use @L_E
target moved do at 2 use @L_W
target moved expect @O_S block=observer at 5
target moved expect @O_S powered=false at 6
target moved expect @O_S powered=true during 7..8
target moved expect @O_E powered=true during 7..8
target moved expect @O_W powered=true during 7..8
target moved expect @O_W powered=false at 9

target new powered "an observer pushed while powered lands unpowered and gives no new pulse"
target powered do at 2 use @L_WATCH
target powered do at 3 use @L_PUSH
target powered expect @O_POW block=observer at 6
target powered expect @O_POW powered=false during 6..20
target powered expect @PROBE powered=false during 0..20

target new gap "a sticky piston with air in front pulls the block beyond its head even after a 2-tick pulse"
target gap do at 2 use @L_GAP
target gap do at 4 use @L_GAP
target gap expect @GAP_FROM block=air during 5..20
target gap expect @GAP_TO block=stone during 7..20

check

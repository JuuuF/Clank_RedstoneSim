# R-007: torch burnout: when it happens and how a torch recovers (doc/redstone/records/R-007-torch-burnout.md)
#
# Rows: P8 and P9: a wall torch on a stone whose lever (on top) switches the stone on
# for 4 ticks every 8 (P8, 8 times) or every 9 (P9, 10 times) ticks. INST and KICK: a
# torch whose own wire (10 wires) loops straight back into its block (it turns itself off
# 2 ticks after it turns on: period 4); KICK gets a stone placed beside its torch at tick
# 200. DELAY: a torch whose loop runs through a delay-1 repeater (period 8). The loops
# start by themselves from the build.
#   redsim-cli r007.redsim.json < experiments/redstone/R-007-torch-burnout.cli.txt

# R-008: what an observer sees (doc/redstone/records/R-008-observers.md)
#
# Rows 4 blocks apart, an observer O_x at (0,1,z) watching west (out=east) the cell
# W_x at (-1,1,z): a wire whose power changes level (WIRELVL), a wire fed by a comparator
# whose strength changes 3 -> 7 (COMPSTR), the comparator itself (COMPD), a barrel getting
# items (BARREL), a repeater being clicked (REPDELAY), air where a stone is placed and
# broken (PLACE), the cell in front of a piston (PISTON), a stone beside a lamp that turns
# on (NOCHANGE); and a chain of three observers each watching the previous (CHAIN).
#   redsim-cli r008.redsim.json < experiments/redstone/R-008-observers.cli.txt
new size 48 force
note "R-008: what observers see: block state changes only (not block-entity data), 2 ticks per observer in a chain"
place 0 1 -22 observer out=east
label O_WIRELVL 0 1 -22
label W_WIRELVL -1 1 -22
place -1 1 -22 redstone_wire
place -2 1 -22 redstone_wire
place -3 1 -22 lever face=floor
place -1 1 -23 lever face=floor
label LA_WIRELVL -3 1 -22
label LB_WIRELVL -1 1 -23
source -3 1 -18 barrel 3
label SRC_COMPSTR -3 1 -18
place -2 1 -18 comparator out=east
label C_COMPSTR -2 1 -18
place 0 1 -18 observer facing=west
label O_COMPSTR 0 1 -18
place -1 1 -18 redstone_wire
label W_COMPSTR -1 1 -18
source -2 1 -14 barrel 3
label SRC_COMPD -2 1 -14
place -1 1 -14 comparator out=east
label C_COMPD -1 1 -14
place 0 1 -14 observer out=east
label O_COMPD 0 1 -14
place 0 1 -10 observer out=east
label O_BARREL 0 1 -10
label W_BARREL -1 1 -10
place -1 1 -10 barrel
place 0 1 -6 observer out=east
label O_REPDELAY 0 1 -6
label W_REPDELAY -1 1 -6
place -1 1 -6 repeater out=north
place 0 1 -2 observer out=east
label O_PLACE 0 1 -2
label W_PLACE -1 1 -2
place 0 1 2 observer out=east
label O_PISTON 0 1 2
label W_PISTON -1 1 2
place -2 1 2 piston facing=east
place -3 1 2 lever face=floor
label L_PISTON -3 1 2
place -1 1 6 redstone_wire
place -2 1 6 lever face=floor
label L_CHAIN -2 1 6
place 0 1 6 observer out=east
place 1 1 6 observer out=east
place 2 1 6 observer out=east
label O_CHAIN1 0 1 6
label O_CHAIN2 1 1 6
label O_CHAIN3 2 1 6
place 0 1 10 observer out=east
label O_NOCHANGE 0 1 10
label W_NOCHANGE -1 1 10
place -1 1 10 stone
place -2 1 10 redstone_lamp
place -3 1 10 lever face=floor
label L_NOCHANGE -3 1 10
settle

target new sees "state changes fire it: wire power levels (also from a comparator), a repeater's delay, a block placed or broken, a piston head appearing and leaving"
target sees do at 2 use @LA_WIRELVL
target sees do at 10 use @LB_WIRELVL
target sees do at 20 use @LB_WIRELVL
target sees do at 10 source @SRC_COMPSTR barrel 7
target sees do at 10 use @W_REPDELAY
target sees do at 10 place @W_PLACE stone
target sees do at 20 break @W_PLACE
target sees do at 10 use @L_PISTON
target sees do at 30 use @L_PISTON
target sees expect @W_WIRELVL power=14 at 2
target sees expect @W_WIRELVL power=15 at 10
target sees expect @O_WIRELVL powered=true during 4..5
target sees expect @O_WIRELVL powered=true during 12..13
target sees expect @O_WIRELVL powered=true during 22..23
target sees expect @W_COMPSTR power=7 at 12
target sees expect @O_COMPSTR powered=true during 14..15
target sees expect @O_REPDELAY powered=true during 12..13
target sees expect @O_PLACE powered=true during 12..13
target sees expect @O_PLACE powered=true during 22..23
target sees expect @O_PISTON powered=false at 12
target sees expect @O_PISTON powered=true during 13..14
target sees expect @O_PISTON powered=false during 15..32
target sees expect @O_PISTON powered=true during 33..34

target new blind "no state change, no pulse: a comparator's strength (3 -> 7, still powered), a barrel's contents, a stone whose neighbor lamp lights"
target blind do at 10 source @SRC_COMPD barrel 7
target blind do at 10 container @W_BARREL 0 stone 64
target blind do at 10 use @L_NOCHANGE
target blind expect @C_COMPD power=7 at 12
target blind expect @W_BARREL analog=1 at 10
target blind expect @O_COMPD powered=false during 0..30
target blind expect @O_BARREL powered=false during 0..30
target blind expect @O_NOCHANGE powered=false during 0..30

target new chain "each observer in a chain adds 2 ticks"
target chain do at 2 use @L_CHAIN
target chain expect @O_CHAIN1 powered=true during 4..5
target chain expect @O_CHAIN2 powered=true during 6..7
target chain expect @O_CHAIN3 powered=true during 8..9
target chain expect @O_CHAIN3 powered=false during 10..20

check

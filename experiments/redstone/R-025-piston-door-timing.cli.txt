place 6 1 1 repeater out=north
set 7 2 0 stone
set 8 1 0 stone
place 8 2 0 repeater out=west
label P 6 1 0
label D 6 1 1
label R 8 2 0

place 1 2 4 sticky_piston facing=up
set 1 5 4 stone

set 2 3 4 stone
set 3 2 4 stone

label K 1 1 5

target headqc do pulse @R at 2 for 1
target headqc expect @P extended=true at 6
target headqc expect 6 2 0 block=piston_head at 6
target headqc expect @P block=moving_piston at 7
target headqc expect 6 2 0 block=stone at 9

target landing do pulse @YR at 2 for 20
target landing do pulse @K at 2 for 30
target landing expect 1 3 4 extended=false at 6

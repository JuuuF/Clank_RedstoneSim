# R-023: instant piston wire (doc/redstone/records/R-023-instant-piston-wire.md)
# A chain of extended sticky pistons, each holding a redstone block against the back of the
# next one, collapses in a single tick when the first one moves: a moved block's old cell is
# updated inside the piston's block event, and the piston it depowers queues its own retract
# in the same block-event phase. Re-extending takes 3 ticks a stage.
#   Row A (z=1): five stages 3 blocks apart (pistons 1+3k 1 1), probe wires 3+3k 1 2.
#   Row B (z=6): three stages linked by 14-wire lines (pistons 1, 18, 35): 38 blocks.
#   Row C (y=2, z=10): the input pushes the first block away (instant on its rising edge);
#     the last stage pulls a stone off the step from the upper wire 12 2 10 down to the
#     output wire 11 1 10, which lights at once.
# Same layout as the game-server scenario instant_piston_wire (G: matched tick by tick).
#   redsim-cli r023.redsim.json < experiments/redstone/R-023-instant-piston-wire.cli.txt
new size 96 force
note "R-023: a sticky-piston chain collapses in one tick (one edge crosses any length at once); re-extending is 3 ticks a stage"
# Row A
place 1 1 1 sticky_piston facing=east
set 2 1 1 redstone_block
place 3 1 2 redstone_wire
label A0 3 1 2 "probe after stage 0"
place 4 1 1 sticky_piston facing=east
set 5 1 1 redstone_block
place 6 1 2 redstone_wire
label A1 6 1 2 "probe after stage 1"
place 7 1 1 sticky_piston facing=east
set 8 1 1 redstone_block
place 9 1 2 redstone_wire
label A2 9 1 2 "probe after stage 2"
place 10 1 1 sticky_piston facing=east
set 11 1 1 redstone_block
place 12 1 2 redstone_wire
label A3 12 1 2 "probe after stage 3"
place 13 1 1 sticky_piston facing=east
set 14 1 1 redstone_block
place 15 1 2 redstone_wire
label A4 15 1 2 "probe after stage 4"
place 0 1 1 lever face=floor
label LA 0 1 1 "input A (lever)"
# Row B
place 1 1 6 sticky_piston facing=east
set 2 1 6 redstone_block
line 4 1 6 to 17 1 6 redstone_wire
place 18 1 6 sticky_piston facing=east
set 19 1 6 redstone_block
line 21 1 6 to 34 1 6 redstone_wire
place 35 1 6 sticky_piston facing=east
set 36 1 6 redstone_block
place 38 1 6 redstone_wire
label B0 4 1 6 "probe after stage 0"
label B1 21 1 6 "probe after stage 1 (block 21)"
label B2 38 1 6 "probe after stage 2 (block 38)"
place 0 1 6 lever face=floor
label LB 0 1 6 "input B (lever)"
# Row C
set 2 2 10 redstone_block
set 4 2 10 redstone_block
set 7 2 10 redstone_block
set 10 2 10 stone
set 12 1 10 stone
set 13 2 10 redstone_block
place 12 2 10 redstone_wire
place 11 1 10 redstone_wire
place 10 1 10 redstone_wire
place 5 1 10 redstone_wire
place 8 1 10 redstone_wire
place 2 2 11 sticky_piston facing=north
place 3 2 10 sticky_piston facing=east
place 6 2 10 sticky_piston facing=east
place 9 2 10 sticky_piston facing=east
set 1 1 11 stone
place 1 2 11 lever face=floor
label LC 1 2 11 "input C (lever)"
label C1 5 1 10 "probe under stage 1's block"
label C2 8 1 10 "probe under stage 2's block"
label CUT 11 2 10 "the stone over the step"
label COUT 11 1 10 "output C: the lower wire of the step"
use @LA
use @LB
settle

target new collapse "inputs at 10: rows A and B lose power, row C gets it: every probe of A and B is off at 11, C's output is on at 11"
target collapse do at 10 use @LA
target collapse do at 10 use @LB
target collapse do at 10 use @LC
target collapse expect @A0 power>=1 during 0..10
target collapse expect @A0 power=0 during 11..24
target collapse expect @A1 power>=1 during 0..10
target collapse expect @A1 power=0 during 11..24
target collapse expect @A2 power>=1 during 0..10
target collapse expect @A2 power=0 during 11..24
target collapse expect @A3 power>=1 during 0..10
target collapse expect @A3 power=0 during 11..24
target collapse expect @A4 power>=1 during 0..10
target collapse expect @A4 power=0 during 11..24
target collapse expect @B0 power>=1 during 0..10
target collapse expect @B0 power=0 during 11..24
target collapse expect @B1 power>=1 during 0..10
target collapse expect @B1 power=0 during 11..24
target collapse expect @B2 power>=1 during 0..10
target collapse expect @B2 power=0 during 11..24
target collapse expect @C1 power>=1 during 0..10
target collapse expect @C1 power=0 during 11..24
target collapse expect @C2 power>=1 during 0..10
target collapse expect @C2 power=0 during 11..24
target collapse expect @COUT power=0 during 0..10
target collapse expect @COUT power>=1 during 11..24

target new reset "inputs back at 10: each stage re-extends 3 ticks after the one before (A on at 13, 16, 19, 22, 25); C's output goes off when the stone is back, 12 ticks"
target reset do use @LA
target reset do use @LB
target reset do use @LC
target reset do at 10 use @LA
target reset do at 10 use @LB
target reset do at 10 use @LC
target reset expect @A0 power=0 during 4..12
target reset expect @A0 power>=1 during 13..40
target reset expect @A1 power=0 during 4..15
target reset expect @A1 power>=1 during 16..40
target reset expect @A2 power=0 during 4..18
target reset expect @A2 power>=1 during 19..40
target reset expect @A3 power=0 during 4..21
target reset expect @A3 power>=1 during 22..40
target reset expect @A4 power=0 during 4..24
target reset expect @A4 power>=1 during 25..40
target reset expect @B0 power=0 during 4..12
target reset expect @B0 power>=1 during 13..40
target reset expect @B1 power=0 during 4..15
target reset expect @B1 power>=1 during 16..40
target reset expect @B2 power=0 during 4..18
target reset expect @B2 power>=1 during 19..40
target reset expect @COUT power>=1 during 4..21
target reset expect @COUT power=0 during 22..40

check

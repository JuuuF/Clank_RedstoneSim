# Work in progress: a 3x3 flush piston door (backlog Q18)

**Done 2026-10-18:** the library module is `examples/circuits/door_3x3` (contract in
doc/redstone/circuits.md, "3x3 flush door"; rules in record R-025). The stages below show how
it got there, including the failures.

Worlds to open in the editor (`redsim FILE`) or inspect with `redsim-cli FILE …`. Each has a
`note` saying what it shows. Rebuild them all with
`python experiments/wip/door3x3/scripts/export_wip.py` (the door itself is generated by
`scripts/door.py mech|ctrl`).

## The mechanism (doorway x 0..2, y 4..6, z 0; floor top y 3)

- **Sides and top** (the easy 7 blocks): three sticky pistons per side in the wall plane
  (x −2 facing east, x 4 facing west) pull their blocks into the wall; one sticky piston
  above (1 8 0, facing down) pulls the top-middle block up into the frame. Each side is
  powered by a wire staircase in the wall plane: the lowest piston through the block behind
  it (wire on top of it), the middle one by a wire pointing into it, the top one through the
  block above it. The left staircase continues over the frame into the top piston. One glass
  step keeps a support block from cutting a wire step.
- **The center column** (the hard 2 blocks): C (center) and M (bottom middle) rest in the
  floor when open: C at 1 3 0, M at 2 3 0. Below C: sticky piston B (1 2 0) on sticky piston
  A (1 1 0); W (3 3 0) pushes M west.
  - Close: B gets a 2-tick pulse and **spits** C up one (C stays, the head comes back); W
    pushes M under C; B pushes M and C up together and holds them.
  - Open: B retracts and pulls M down; W pulls M aside; A lifts B into the floor; B
    (powered through the floor block 0 3 0) extends into the gap and pulls C down one; A
    lowers B; B extends into the gap again and pulls C into the floor.
- When closed, B's head and W's head are the floor under the door; when open, C and M are.

## The controller (stage 04)

- A wire trunk (z = 5, y = 2) carries the input; short repeater branches north of it make
  the delayed signals; every piston driver is a delay-1 repeater (a block under W, the floor
  block 0 3 0 for B raised).
- **Spit:** an observer watches a repeater on a trunk spur; its 2-tick pulse goes through B's
  driver (a repeater keeps 2-tick pulses; a comparator edge detector would give 4 or more,
  which pulls C back instead of spitting it). It also fires on the falling edge, but B's hold
  is still on then and covers it.
- **W's asymmetric timing:** W must turn on before B's hold and off after it. W's driver is
  **locked** by a repeater fed 2 ticks after the hold: locked while B holds, it ignores its
  input turning off, and when the lock releases it re-reads the input and turns off. No second
  delay line is needed.
- **A's pulse:** a subtracting comparator drives A directly: rear 28 ticks after the input,
  side 18 (both via repeater chains), so it gives a 10-tick pulse on the falling edge only.
- **B raised and the final pull from one observer:** an observer watches A. A extending gives
  a pulse that reaches the floor block 0 3 0 (B raised extends and pulls C down one); A
  retracting gives a pulse into B's front driver (B home pulls C into the floor). Both pulses
  also reach the other target, harmlessly: A's head at 1 2 0 is not a conductor, and B at
  home is extending anyway.

## Rules found on the way (now in R-025 and the contract)

- A repeater into the upper of two stacked pistons masks the lower one's quasi-connectivity
  (it updates the upper one first; the lower one's push fails on the extended piston).
- A piston held by quasi-connectivity through a block beside its head cell misses the off
  update while the head moves; it retracts when the head lands, one tick late (stage 04's
  final pull, the variants `final=wire` of `door.py fast`).
- A piston set down next to a powered block extends one tick after landing: stage 05 uses it
  for B's hold (B fires when M lands) and for the final pull (B fires when A sets it down),
  which removed B's front driver.
- An observer placed before the wire it watches fires during `settle` (stage 05's first build
  spat C into the doorway).
